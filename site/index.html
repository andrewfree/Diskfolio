BEFORE YOU SCAN

Good to know.

Where does Diskfolio look?

It inspects app bundles in Applications and ~/Applications, including apps in subfolders. The selection and analysis run on your Mac.

Do the bars show space I can free?

Diskfolio checks one temporary copy at a time and shows the logical bytes in apps that pass signature verification. The app rows share a separate scale so you can compare them. Actual reclaimed space depends on APFS and how the apps are packaged. Free Space lets you review the verified apps before applying changes.

How does Diskfolio request permission?

If macOS asks to allow app updates, respond to its prompt. The Free Space screen opens App Management in System Settings and gives you a Diskfolio icon to drag into the app list.

How do I build Diskfolio?

Run ./build-app.sh from the repository on an Apple Silicon Mac with macOS 13 or later and the Xcode command-line developer tools. Open build/Diskfolio.app when the script finishes.
